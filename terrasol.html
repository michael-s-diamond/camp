TerraSol

Design a star-planet system and explore the physics behind planetary habitability. This is a static, client-side rebuild of TerraSol (originally by Andre Perkins and Michael Diamond, previously hosted on Heroku) — no server required, so it can't go down the way the old version did.

Planetary habitability simulator

Surface temperature as a function of albedo and greenhouse effect, for the current solar input. Colored bands follow the original app's temperature scale (32–112°F).

Physics ported from the original TerraSol source (Stefan-Boltzmann blackbody radiation, inverse-square law, and the SimpleClimate surface-temperature model). The heavier latitude-resolved energy balance model in the original repo was never actually part of the deployed app, so it isn't reproduced here.
